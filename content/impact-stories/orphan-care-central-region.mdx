Some forms of charity are measured in what is delivered. Orphan care must be measured in something deeper: whether a child feels safe enough to grow.

An Nur’s care model holds practical support and emotional dignity together. Food, shelter, education, faith, and daily consistency all matter because children need more than survival.

## The dignity of consistency

For vulnerable children, stability is not a luxury. It is the ground beneath every other form of development.

> A child who feels protected can begin to imagine a future again.

This story is a reminder that social welfare is not separate from development. It is often the place where development begins.
