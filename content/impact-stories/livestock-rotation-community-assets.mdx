Some support is consumed once. Some support keeps moving.

The livestock rotation model is built around the second idea. A productive asset can help one family today, then become part of a wider cycle of benefit tomorrow.

## Assets with accountability

An Nur’s model asks a deeper question: how can support create ownership without isolating families from the community?

> The goal is not charity that stops. The goal is value that circulates.

Livestock rotation gives families a practical asset base while preserving a shared sense of responsibility. It is simple, human, and powerful when handled with trust.
